# 📊 Vendor Performance Analysis

## Overview
This project integrates **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** in Jupyter with an **interactive Power BI dashboard** to provide a complete view of vendor and brand performance.  
It focuses on **sales, purchases, profit margins, and vendor contributions**, enabling clear insights into top performers, underperformers, and overall profitability.

---

## ✨ Key Features (Explained in Detail)

### 1. Summary KPIs
At the top of the dashboard, five key metrics summarize overall performance:
- **Total Sales ($451.62M)** – Total revenue generated across all vendors.  
- **Total Purchases ($321.90M)** – Procurement costs across vendors.  
- **Gross Profit ($134.07M)** – Profit after subtracting purchases from sales.  
- **Profit Margin (40.48%)** – Efficiency measure showing profitability percentage.  
- **Unsold Capital ($2.71M)** – Value of inventory that remains unsold, highlighting potential waste.

### 2. Purchase Contribution (%) Donut Chart
- Shows each vendor’s share of total purchases.  
- Example: **DIAGEO NORTH AMERICA INC** contributes ~24.81%, making it the largest procurement partner.  
- Helps identify dominant suppliers and potential risks of dependency.

### 3. Top Vendors by Sales
- Bar chart ranking vendors by revenue.  
- Example: **DIAGEO NORTH AMERICA INC** leads with ~$69M, followed by Martignetti Companies and Pernod Ricard USA.  
- Highlights vendors driving the most revenue.

### 4. Bottom Vendors by Sales
- Bar chart showing vendors with the lowest sales.  
- Example: **Black Rock Spirits** and **American Spirit** generate only a few thousand dollars.  
- Useful for spotting inefficiencies and underperforming suppliers.

### 5. Top Brands by Sales
- Chart ranking brands by revenue contribution.  
- Helps identify which product lines are most successful and which need improvement.

### 6. Profit Margin Analysis
- Provides insights into profitability across vendors and brands.  
- Ensures decisions are based not just on sales volume but also on actual profit contribution.
